Splash screen · v1

BGYM opening animation

What members see when they tap the app icon. It plays once (about 2.5 seconds), then hands off to sign-in. Returning members who are already signed in go straight to Home.

  1. 0.0 sGlow and dot. A warm orange glow lights the screen and the orange dot appears.
  2. 0.25 sThe athlete rises into place with a small bounce.
  3. 0.6 sThe orbit draws around his waist while the dot travels one full lap, passing behind him and back in front.
  4. 0.7 sHe lifts: the dumbbells push up once.
  5. 1.2 sB · G · Y · M rise in one letter at a time, orange B first.
  6. 1.6 s“Be my gym buddy.” fades in.
  7. 2.6 sHand-off: the splash fades and the sign-in screen slides up.

Colours, logo and font are from the BGYM brand kit. Motion is reduced automatically for people who turn on “Reduce motion” on their phone.
